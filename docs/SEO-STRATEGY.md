# SEO strategy — Aravindh Invisible Grills

Primary topic: **Invisible Grills**. Primary market: **Hyderabad**, with focus on
**Nallagandla** and **Vanasthalipuram**. This strategy is original to Aravindh; competitor
names are used only for analysis, never as our keywords or branding.

## 1. Competitor analysis (Nandhan Invisible Grills & similar)

Observed patterns from established Hyderabad competitors:

| Area | What competitors do | How we compete (originally) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Title structure** | "`<Service>` in Hyderabad" and long-tail variants ("…fixing near me", "…price", "…dealers"). | Unique "`<Keyword>` in Hyderabad \| Aravindh Invisible Grills" titles on every page, plus dedicated material pages (SS304/SS316) and solution pages (Child Safety). |
| **Service granularity** | Many separate, indexable service pages. | 28 individual service pages + 3 guides, each with its own metadata, H1 and content. |
| **Location targeting** | City + branch lists. | Genuinely useful location pages (Hyderabad, Nallagandla, Vanasthalipuram) describing local housing types and needs — not thin doorway pages. We do **not** invent branch addresses. |
| **Internal linking** | "Our Services" lists and "you may also need" blocks. | Related-service links on every service page, guide cross-links, footer hub links, and contextual links from the home page. |
| **Content quality** | Keyword-led paragraphs, FAQ blocks. | Original, specific copy (grade differences, local housing context, honest trade-offs) plus FAQ schema where unique. |
| **Structured data** | LocalBusiness with geo. | Organization + LocalBusiness, WebSite, Service, BreadcrumbList and FAQPage in one consistent `@graph`. |

**Gaps we exploit:** a clear SS304-vs-SS316 explainer, a whole-home Child Safety Solutions page,
consistent brand identity (logo/favicon/OG), clean canonicals and a verified sitemap, and
server-rendered metadata on every URL.

## 3. Internal linking architecture

- **Home** links to all services, the three location pages, and the SS304/SS316/Child-Safety/About guides.
- **Service pages** link to related services (same category + key cross-sells) and, for grills, to the SS304/SS316 guides.
- **Guide pages** link to the relevant services and to each other (SS304 ↔ SS316).
- **Location pages** link to popular services and to the other locations.
- **Footer** (every page) is a hub: Invisible Grills, Safety Nets & Bird Control, Company & Service Areas.
- Breadcrumbs (visible + `BreadcrumbList` schema) on every inner page.

## 4. Content quality rules

- One H1 per page; unique title and meta description (enforced by the build).
- Location pages describe real local context (housing types, common problems) — never spun duplicates.
- Honest, non-overclaiming copy: "helps prevent", "ask our team to confirm warranty", grade trade-offs explained.
- Descriptive, keyword-relevant ALT text on service images.

## 5. Off-page & local recommendations (next steps, outside this codebase)

1. **Google Business Profile**: create/verify a profile for the service area; keep name, phone and hours
   identical to the site (NAP consistency). Add real project photos and services.
2. **Reviews**: request genuine Google reviews from past customers; only then display testimonials on-site.
3. **Citations**: consistent listings on Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART and local directories.
4. **Backlinks**: local community/apartment associations, supplier listings, and genuine guest posts.
5. **Photos**: replace any reference imagery with your own installation photos over time; keep ALT text accurate.
6. **Content expansion**: add case studies and a balcony-safety buying guide; consider one strong page per
   high-value locality only when it can carry genuinely unique content.

## 6. Measurement

Track in Search Console: impressions/clicks/position for the keyword map above, indexed-page count,
Core Web Vitals and mobile usability. Review monthly and expand content where queries show demand.
